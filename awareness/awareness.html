Water Usage Based On Quality

Drinking Water

Water with balanced pH and safe turbidity is suitable for drinking and cooking.

Agricultural Water

Moderate quality water can be used for irrigation and farming.

Industrial Water

High mineral or treated water can be reused in industries and cooling systems.

Unsafe Water

Highly polluted water should not be used for human consumption.
