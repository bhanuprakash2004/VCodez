Smart Water Quality System

Monitor Water. Understand Quality. Use It Wisely.

This project helps users check water quality using pH, turbidity, and temperature. It gives instant results, charts, records, and usage suggestions for better water management.

⚡

Instant Checking

Users can check water quality immediately without waiting for slow lab reports.

📊

Visual Analysis

Charts help users understand pH, turbidity, and temperature values clearly.

💧

Usage Suggestion

The system suggests whether water is useful for drinking, surface water, agriculture, or industry.

Why This Website Is Useful

Rapid urbanization and pollution can affect water quality. This website provides a simple digital solution to monitor water, store records, compare samples, and support better water usage decisions.
