About This System

This application helps monitor water quality using scientific water parameters. Users can analyze water conditions instantly and understand whether water is suitable for drinking, agriculture, industrial use, or other purposes.

Water Quality Parameters

pH Level

pH measures how acidic or alkaline water is.

Safe Range: 6.5 – 8.5

Low pH causes corrosion while high pH affects taste and safety.

Turbidity

Turbidity represents how clean or dirty water is.

Safe Range: Less than 5 NTU

High turbidity may indicate contamination, bacteria, or suspended particles.

Temperature

Temperature affects bacterial growth and overall water condition.

Cold • Moderate • Warm

If pH and turbidity are safe, water can still be drinkable even if cold or warm.

How The System Determines Water Usage

Drinking Water

If pH and turbidity are within safe range, water is suitable for drinking, cooking, and domestic use.

Agricultural Water

Moderate quality water can still be useful for farming, irrigation, and gardening.

Industrial Water

Water with higher turbidity or minerals may still be used for industrial operations and cooling systems.

Unsafe Water

Highly contaminated water should not be used directly and requires proper treatment.

Water Awareness

Clean water is a limited resource. Smart conservation and proper usage help protect future generations.
