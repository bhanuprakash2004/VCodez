💧 Water Quality Monitoring System

Analyze water safety using pH, Turbidity, and Temperature

About This System

This application helps in analyzing water quality in real-time using basic parameters. It allows users to check whether water is safe or unsafe based on scientific conditions.

🔬 Water Quality Parameters

🧪 pH Level

pH measures how acidic or alkaline water is.

Safe Range: 6.5 – 8.5

Low pH can cause corrosion, while high pH affects taste and safety.

🌫️ Turbidity

Turbidity shows how clear or dirty the water is.

Safe Range: Less than 5 NTU

High turbidity means dirt, bacteria, or contamination.

🌡️ Temperature

Temperature affects water quality and bacterial growth.

Normal Range: 20°C – 30°C

Higher temperature can increase harmful microorganisms.

⚙️ How Quality is Determined

The system evaluates water quality using all three parameters:

  • ✔ pH between 6.5 and 8.5
  • ✔ Turbidity less than or equal to 5 NTU
  • ✔ Temperature within safe range

If all conditions are satisfied → Good Water ✅ Otherwise → Poor Water ⚠️
